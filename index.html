Questions

Good to know.

Can't find your answer? Write to us and a real person replies within a day.

Does it taste chalky like other protein shakes?

No. The protein comes from filtered milk, not powder, so there is nothing to dissolve. It drinks like a cold chocolate milkshake, smooth all the way down.

What are BCAAs, and why are they in there?

BCAAs are three amino acids: leucine, isoleucine and valine. Your muscles use them to repair after training. Milk protein is naturally rich in them, and filtering keeps them in.

When should I drink it?

Within an hour after training works well. Plenty of people also have one at breakfast or as an afternoon snack. It's food, so drink it when you're hungry.

Does it need to stay in the fridge?

Unopened bottles keep in the cupboard for months. Once opened, keep it cold and finish it within 24 hours. It tastes best chilled either way.
